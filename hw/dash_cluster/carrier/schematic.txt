DASH NODE CARRIER ("piggyback") - schematic sketch
==================================================

A small board that plugs into the display module's 8-pin 0.1" header and
gives it a car supply, a CAN transceiver and a config EEPROM. One design,
built three times; which node it is comes out of the EEPROM, so the three
boards are identical.

Drawn 2026-09-20 from the decisions in PLAN.md sections 4.1, 4.9 and 4.13.

THE NETLIST OF RECORD IS gen_schematic.py, which writes carrier.kicad_sch -
open that in KiCad. This file is the reasoning behind it: why each part is
there, and what must not be changed without knowing why. Values here are
starting points; anything marked (opt) can be left unpopulated.

KiCad's ERC passes on the generated file with no errors and no warnings, and
the netlist was read back and checked connection by connection - which is how
the Schottky and the gate Zener were caught in backwards.

THE CONNECTOR, as the display module presents it
------------------------------------------------

    J1  1  IO29   - leave unconnected. The AXP2101 PMIC asserts this.
        2  IO28   - I2C SDA, to the config EEPROM  (U3, R5)
        3  IO27   - I2C SCL, to the config EEPROM  (U3, R6)
        4  RXD    - GPIO25, CAN receive            <- U2 pin 4
        5  TXD    - GPIO26, CAN transmit           -> R1 -> U2 pin 1
        6  3V3    - the module's 3.3 V, an OUTPUT  -> U2 pin 5 (VIO)
        7  GND    - the one ground
        8  VBUS   - 5 V IN, the node's supply      <- D2

POWER
-----

  12 V ignition
   (loom pin 1)
        |
       [F1] 1 A                      Reverse polarity: a P-FET, not a diode.
        |                            No drop to lose when cranking.
        +--------+
        |        |          Q1  IRLML6402 or similar P-ch, -20 V
        |      [D1]         ,-----------------------.
        |      TVS          |  S                  D |
        |    SMBJ26A        +---. .-----------------+---------+------> VIN
        |       |           |   | |                           |
       GND     GND          |  [R2] 100k                    [C1] 10u
                            |   | |                           |       25 V
                            |   +-+--[ZD1] 12 V (opt)         |
                            |   |                            GND
                            |  GND   (gate pulled to source
                            |         through R2; ZD1 caps
                            |         Vgs on a 24 V jump start)
                            |
                        (body diode conducts on reverse,
                         so the FET's source is the 12 V side)

    VIN ---> U1  TSR 1-2450  (5 V, 1 A, 6.5-36 V in, 78xx pinout)
             .-------------.
     VIN ----| IN      OUT |---- +5V ----+--------+
             |             |             |        |
             |     GND     |           [C2]     [C3]
             '------+------'            10u      100n
                    |                    |        |
                   GND                  GND      GND

      +5V --+--[D2]--+-----------------------------> J1-8  VBUS
            |  SS34  |                               (the node's supply)
            |        |
            |      [C4] 470u  <- HOLD-UP. This is what buys the firmware
            |        |           time to close the log and save the page
            |       GND          when the ignition goes (PLAN 4.13).
            |
            +---> U2 pin 3 (transceiver VCC)

    D2 matters twice: it stops C4 draining back into the regulator when the
    ignition goes, and it stops a bench USB lead and this regulator fighting
    over VBUS - they are one net on the module (PLAN 4.1).

CAN TRANSCEIVER
---------------

    U2  TJA1051T/3   (the /3 suffix is essential: it has VIO, and the
                      RP2350's pins are NOT 5 V tolerant)

                     .-----------.
      J1-5 TXD --[R1]| 1 TXD   S 8|---- GND     S low = normal mode.
              1k     |            |             NEVER leave it floating:
               GND---| 2 GND CANH 7|---> CANH    it silently stops transmitting.
               +5V---| 3 VCC CANL 6|---> CANL
      J1-4 RXD ------| 4 RXD  VIO 5|---- J1-6 3V3
                     '-----------'

        C5 100n across VCC/GND, C6 100n across VIO/GND, both at the pins.

    Termination - FIT ONLY ON THE TWO BOARDS AT THE ENDS OF THE BUS:

      CANH ---+--[R3 60R]--+--[R4 60R]--+--- CANL
              |            |            |
              |          [C7] 4n7       |
              |            |            |
              |           GND           |     Split termination: the two
            [D3 PESD2CAN or PESD1CAN]         halves with a capacitor to
              |                               ground damp common-mode noise
             GND                              better than a single 120 R.
                                              A single 120 R is acceptable.

    Make it a solder link or a pad pair, not a fixed part. Five nodes on this
    bus and exactly two ends: the bench proved how hard a termination fault
    is to read - every frame failed its own readback and it looked like a
    broken transceiver (PLAN 4.1).

CONFIG EEPROM  (J1-2 SDA, J1-3 SCL)
-----------------------------------

    U3  24LC256 (or 24AA02 - anything in the 24xx family at 0x50)

                     .-----------.
               GND---| 1 A0   VCC 8|---- J1-6 3V3
               GND---| 2 A1    WP 7|---- GND      WP low: the firmware writes it
               GND---| 3 A2   SCL 6|---- J1-3 IO27
               GND---| 4 GND  SDA 5|---- J1-2 IO28
                     '-----------'

      R5 4k7 from SDA to 3V3, R6 4k7 from SCL to 3V3, C9 100n at the part.

    WHAT IT HOLDS, and why it is here rather than in the module's own flash:
    everything that belongs to THIS POSITION IN THE LOOM rather than to the
    display module plugged into it - the node identity, the g-meter's
    mounting angle, whether this board is one of the two with termination
    fitted, a serial number, and the page the driver left it on. Swap a
    display module and the car keeps all of it.

    It is also why IO27 and IO28 are an I2C pair and not the identity divider
    and the illumination sense they were in an earlier draft: the module has
    only three spare pins, the PMIC owns IO29, and the illumination sense is
    moving to another CAN node.

    Bit-banged, driven from a timer rather than a busy wait - see PLAN.md
    4.14a. There is no hardware I2C pair on these two pins.

LOOM  (J2 in, J3 out - the chain passes straight through this board)
-------------------------------------------------------------------

    Four wires, daisy chained between the ECU boards and the three nodes:

        1  12 V (switched ignition)      J2-1 --- J3-1, and to F1
        2  GND                           J2-2 --- J3-2
        3  CANH                          J2-3 --- J3-3, and to U2
        4  CANL                          J2-4 --- J3-4, and to U2

    The node at the end of the chain is the one with nothing in J3, and is
    therefore where the termination above gets fitted.

    The ground wire is not optional: the nodes and the ECU sit metres apart
    and are grounded at different points, and carrying GND alongside the pair
    is what keeps the common-mode offset inside the transceiver's range.

LAYOUT NOTES
------------

  * One ground pour, tied to J1-7. Every ground above is that ground.
  * Keep U1's input loop (VIN, C1, its ground return) tight and away from
    CANH/CANL. It is a switcher a few centimetres from a differential pair.
  * Route CANH/CANL as a pair, same length, no stubs; the termination pads
    at the very end of the pair, not part way along.
  * C4 sits on the J1 side of D2. On the regulator's side it would hold up
    the regulator, which is not the thing that needs holding up.
  * Nothing on IO29.
